First Rhythm Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

First Rhythm is a pregnancy and baby care record for iPhone and iPad, provided by Adam Zemancik. This policy explains how the app handles family information.

Information stored on your device

First Rhythm lets an adult parent or caregiver enter information such as names, dates, pregnancy details, feeding and sleep logs, diaper and medicine records, reminders, family-entered child health records, and parent wellbeing notes.

The app stores a local copy in its private data store so it remains usable offline. First Rhythm does not use a separate app account or a developer-operated server for this information. The developer does not receive or view the local record.

Optional private iCloud sharing

If this feature is available and an eligible parent explicitly enables it, First Rhythm can use Apple CloudKit to share selected information with a person the parent invites. Sharing is optional, is scoped to a particular child, and uses a separate private Apple invitation and access choice for each selected category.

Depending on the categories selected, iCloud may store and synchronize a child profile, pregnancy information, care logs, medicine or supplement entries, reminders, and child health records that are not marked private. Parent wellbeing notes and child health records marked private are excluded. Profile photos are excluded by default.

CloudKit private-database records are not visible to the developer through Apple’s developer portal. Apple processes CloudKit data under its own terms and privacy practices. People invited by the parent can access the categories and permissions granted to them and may retain local copies on their devices.

No advertising, analytics, or tracking

First Rhythm contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK. The developer does not use family or health information for advertising or marketing, track users across apps or websites, sell personal information, or send it to data brokers.

Exports

The app creates a PDF or CSV export only when a user selects an export action. The export is created locally and handled through the iOS or iPadOS sharing interface. The user chooses whether and where to send it. Recipients and services selected by the user may retain a copy under their own terms and privacy practices.

Notifications

Optional reminders use local notifications. Lock-screen notification text is designed not to reveal a child’s name, reminder title, or notes. When private iCloud sharing is available, Apple push services may notify the app that shared records changed; these background notifications are used to request synchronization and do not display family content.

Evidence links

Educational content may include links to public guidance from third-party health organizations. A link opens only after the user selects it. The destination website applies its own privacy policy, and the developer does not control its collection or use of information.

Your controls and deletion

Users can view, edit, export, and delete supported records in the app according to their family role and category permissions. A parent role can use Delete All App Data to remove First Rhythm profiles, journeys, care logs, household members, and reminders from the device.

If private iCloud sharing is active, the owner can stop a category share, remove a participant, or choose whether to delete owned private iCloud category data when deleting local app data. Remote changes require confirmation from iCloud. If iCloud cannot confirm deletion, First Rhythm keeps the local data so the user can retry rather than claiming the remote copy was removed.

Deletion cannot recall an export or a copy another person already saved. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to Apple’s operating-system and device-backup behavior.

Children’s information

First Rhythm is intended for use by adult parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children and does not ask a child to create an account. Adults are responsible for entering or sharing information about a child only when they have the authority to do so and for inviting only trusted recipients.

Health and emergency disclaimer

First Rhythm is a family record and general educational aid. It does not diagnose conditions, assess symptoms, recommend medicine doses, replace professional care, or provide emergency services. Contact your maternity or child-health team with concerns and use local emergency services for urgent help.

Security

First Rhythm uses Apple platform protections for its local app container and, when available, Apple’s private sharing controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free. Protect your device and Apple Account, review every recipient and category, and share exports only with people you trust.
