First Rhythm Support

A calm pregnancy and baby care record for iPhone and iPad.

Health and safety. First Rhythm records what your family enters and provides general educational material. It does not diagnose, assess symptoms, recommend medicine doses, replace professional care, or provide emergency services. Contact your maternity or child-health team with concerns and use local emergency services for urgent help.

Contact

Email info@sdilejslunce.cz for app support, privacy questions, feedback, or feature requests.

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, First Rhythm version and build, the screen or feature involved, what you expected, and what happened. Do not email exports or real family, child, pregnancy, medicine, or health information.

Common help

Does First Rhythm require an account?

No. First Rhythm does not use a separate app account or password. The local record is designed to remain usable offline. Public evidence links require a connection when you choose to open them.

Where is my information stored?

First Rhythm keeps a local copy in the app’s private data store on your device. If optional private iCloud sharing is available and an eligible parent explicitly enables a category, records in that selected category are also stored in Apple CloudKit for the people the parent invites. Parent wellbeing notes and child health records marked private are excluded.

How do I export records?

For a newborn profile, open More. A parent can create a seven-day PDF summary, a CSV for the current child, or a household care CSV. Exports are created only after you choose an export action. Share them only with people you trust.

How do I create or change a reminder?

Open More > Manage reminders for the selected child. Reminders use local notifications. If notifications were denied, open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, find First Rhythm, and review Notifications.

Why is a reminder vague on the lock screen?

First Rhythm intentionally uses generic lock-screen text so a child’s name, reminder title, and notes are not exposed. Open the app to review the reminder details.

How do I delete an individual record?

Open the relevant Journal or Records screen, choose the item, and use its delete action. First Rhythm asks for confirmation before deleting sensitive records.

How do I delete all app data?

For a newborn profile, open More > Delete all app data, read the scope, type DELETE, and confirm. This action is permanent on that device and cannot recall an export or a copy held by another person.

How does private iCloud sharing work?

If the feature is available, an eligible parent can open Family > Family Circle and choose specific categories for a trusted person. Each newly shared category uses a separate private Apple invitation and its own access setting. Cloud sharing should never be treated as a backup, and a successful setup action does not by itself prove that another device received current information.

Privacy or security concerns

Email info@sdilejslunce.cz with “First Rhythm privacy” in the subject. Do not attach exports or include private family or health details. We may ask for non-sensitive device and app-version information needed to investigate.
