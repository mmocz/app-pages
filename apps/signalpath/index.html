Terrain-informed radio planning

Point-to-point links

Place two sites, enter antenna and radio details, and inspect terrain line of sight, 60% Fresnel clearance, obstructions, path loss, and estimated link margin.

Terrain visibility

Place a transmitter and create a full-circle or directional terrain screen using your chosen range, frequency, and transmitter and receiver heights.

Saved planning

Keep sites, targets, equipment profiles, link setups, coverage setups, and frozen results on your device, then compare completed studies.

Useful exports

Create PDF reports and share available results as CSV, GeoJSON, KML, PNG, or concise text for documentation and field work.

Planning aid, not a reception guarantee.

Terrain models do not capture every building, tree, interference source, weather condition, diffraction effect, antenna characteristic, or local rule. Confirm regulations, equipment specifications, and critical links in the field.

Privacy-minded by design

Saved plans and results stay on your device. SignalPath has no advertising and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Terrain analysis uses account, security, and terrain services as described in the privacy policy.
