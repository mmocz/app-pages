SignalPath

Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

SignalPath is a terrain and radio-link planning app for iPhone and iPad, provided by Adam Zemancik. This policy explains how the app and its terrain-analysis service handle information.

Information stored on your device

SignalPath stores saved plans, sites, targets, equipment profiles, radio settings, completed results, App Attest state, a random installation identifier, and account-flow state in the app’s local storage. Exported files are created locally and are provided to another app or service only when you choose a Share or Export destination.

Information used for terrain analysis

When you request a terrain calculation, SignalPath sends the coordinates and technical planning inputs needed for the request, such as sampling count, range, frequency, and antenna heights, to its account, security, and terrain services. These services use Supabase-hosted functions and a SignalPath-operated private terrain service. Depending on the requested area and the terrain source used, elevation processing may also involve Open-Meteo, the Czech Office for Surveying, Mapping and Cadastre (ČÚZK), the United States Geological Survey (USGS), or Copernicus DEM data and infrastructure.

For account, allowance, service-protection, and abuse-prevention functions, SignalPath processes a Supabase account identifier, random installation identifier, App Attest key and assertion material, authentication and session information, analysis reservations, allowance and rate-limit state, and the request information needed to validate and serve a terrain calculation.

Location permission and maps

SignalPath may request precise location while the app is in use, only after you grant iOS location permission. Location is used to center the map or start a plan. SignalPath does not receive a continuous background location feed. You can decline permission and use map taps or manual coordinates instead.

The app displays Apple Maps content and may use Apple map imagery in an optional PDF export. Apple processes map requests under its own privacy policy.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional and provides a larger daily terrain-analysis allowance. SignalPath requests no name or email scope from Apple. The service receives the authentication information needed to establish and protect the account. If you delete an Apple-backed SignalPath account, the app requests fresh Apple authorization, revokes SignalPath’s Apple access, and then deletes the account.

How information is used

SignalPath uses information to provide map and terrain calculations, maintain saved planning data on your device, authenticate and manage accounts, enforce analysis allowances and service limits, protect the service from fraud and abuse, support account deletion, and troubleshoot service reliability and security.

SignalPath does not sell personal information, display advertising, use personal information for cross-app or cross-site advertising, or track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Sharing and service providers

Information is disclosed only as needed to operate the feature you request: to Apple for Sign in with Apple, App Attest, and map services; to Supabase and SignalPath-operated services for account, security, allowance, and terrain processing; and to an applicable elevation provider when a terrain request uses that provider. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service.

Retention and deletion

Saved planning data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Account, device-verification, allowance, and account-linked service records are retained while the account is active. Short-lived security challenges expire after five minutes. Unfinished terrain-analysis reservations expire after five minutes, while completed reservation and allowance records remain linked to the active account for allowance enforcement and service security.

Deleting an account from the Account screen permanently deletes its Supabase Auth identity and account-linked SignalPath records. For abuse prevention, SignalPath retains only the random installation identifier and consumed allowance count until the current daily or weekly allowance resets; this retained quota hold contains no user ID, App Attest credential, coordinate, terrain request, or result. Security audit events may remain after deletion in de-identified form without the deleted user or installation identifier.

Operational service logs may contain technical connection information such as IP address, request time, response status, and safe error or timing information. Sensitive authorization and assertion headers are redacted. Operational logs and provider backups are retained according to security, reliability, log-rotation, and backup schedules, then deleted or overwritten when no longer needed, except where longer retention is required for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Deleting the app removes app-controlled local storage subject to iOS backup and system behavior. It does not remove files you previously exported or shared with another destination.

Your choices

You can decline location permission, place planning points manually, delete saved items from the app, choose whether to sign in with Apple, and request account deletion from the Account screen. To make a privacy request or ask a question, email info@sdilejslunce.cz.

International processing

SignalPath’s service providers and elevation services may process information in countries other than your own. Their locations and safeguards are governed by their respective terms and privacy notices.

Children

SignalPath is not directed to children. Do not use SignalPath if you are below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in your location without a parent or guardian’s involvement.

Security

SignalPath uses Apple App Attest, authenticated service requests, system transport security, access controls, and request limits to protect the service. No storage or transmission method is completely risk-free.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its effective date. App and App Store disclosures will also be updated when data handling changes.
