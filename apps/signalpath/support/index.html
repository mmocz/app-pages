Important limitation.

SignalPath is a terrain-planning aid, not a guarantee of reception or coverage. Terrain resolution and model type vary by location. Confirm equipment specifications, local radio rules, and critical links in the field.

Getting started

  1. Open Map and choose Link path.
  2. Place Site A and Site B by tapping the map or entering latitude and longitude.
  3. Enter antenna heights and choose a radio preset or custom frequency.
  4. Select Analyze point-to-point link.
  5. Review terrain line of sight, 60% Fresnel clearance, obstructions, link-budget estimates, and terrain-source details.

For a terrain-visibility study, switch to Coverage, place the transmitter site, choose range and direction, enter transmitter and receiver heights, and select Show terrain visibility.

Common questions

What uses an analysis?

Each new point-to-point terrain profile or coverage request uses one analysis. Failed terrain-service operations are refunded, although temporary rate limits can still apply. Editing plans, viewing or comparing saved results, optimizing heights, and exporting files do not use an analysis.

A verified guest device currently receives 5 terrain analyses per day. Optional Sign in with Apple provides 10 per day. To protect service reliability, one new analysis may start per minute and up to five per rolling hour. The app displays the authoritative current allowance and reset time.

Is location permission required?

No. Location access is optional and is used only while the app is open to center the map or start a plan. If you decline, you can tap the map or enter coordinates manually. SignalPath does not use continuous background location.

Where are saved plans and exports kept?

Saved sites, plans, equipment profiles, and completed results are stored on your device. An export remains on your device unless you choose to share it with another app or service. Removing SignalPath removes app-controlled local storage subject to iOS backup and system behavior, but does not remove files previously exported elsewhere.

Why might a terrain result differ from field conditions?

A terrain model may include or omit buildings and vegetation depending on its source. Weather, interference, diffraction, antenna configuration, equipment performance, and local obstructions also affect real links. Every completed result identifies its terrain source and provides applicable notes and attribution.

Sign in with Apple and account deletion

Sign in with Apple is optional. SignalPath does not request your name or email address from Apple.

To delete the current account:

  1. Open Map.
  2. Tap the person icon in the top toolbar.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Account deletion permanently removes the associated server account and account-linked service records. Apple-backed deletion also revokes SignalPath’s Sign in with Apple access. Locally saved plans remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app.

Accessibility

SignalPath uses native iPhone and iPad controls, supports Dynamic Type, provides text and symbols in addition to result colors, and presents the terrain-profile chart to VoiceOver with a textual summary. Map points can be entered as coordinates or from current location without relying on map taps alone.

If an accessibility feature prevents you from completing a planning task, email info@sdilejslunce.cz with the device, operating-system version, accessibility feature, and affected task.
