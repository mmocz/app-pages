Svalbard Map Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Svalbard Map is an offline map and track-recording aid for iPhone. This policy applies to the app and this support website.

Information the app uses

  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your location to show your position, centre the map, and record a track you choose to start. During an active recording, it can continue receiving location updates when the screen is locked or you use another app. iOS shows its location-use indicator during this use. The app does not use location in the background when you are not recording.
  • Tracks, places and offline maps. Recorded tracks, saved places, downloaded map tiles and saved-area details are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to the developer.
  • Imported GPX files. A GPX file is read only when you select it for import. Its route is displayed in the app and is not sent to the developer.

Map-service requests

When a map tile is not already saved, or you choose online-only satellite, orthophoto, nautical-chart or depth-data imagery, the app requests it from the Norwegian Polar Institute (NPI) or Kartverket map service. Like other web services, either provider may receive technical connection information such as an IP address and request time, and may infer the requested map area from the tile request. The app does not send your GPS location, recorded track, saved places or GPX-file contents to either provider. Their handling of service requests is governed by their own terms and privacy information.

Orthophoto, nautical-chart and depth-data images are not saved as offline maps. Recently viewed images may remain temporarily cached for up to 21 days, subject to a shared 150 MB limit and iOS storage reclamation.

What the app does not do

Svalbard Map does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking, developer-operated cloud backups, or sale of personal information.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline or revoke location permission in iPhone Settings and stop a recording at any time. You can delete tracks, saved places and offline map areas in the app. Deleting the app removes its private on-device data, subject to iOS backup settings you control. Because the developer does not receive a copy of this on-device data, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it for you.
