Svalbard Map Support

Offline-first maps and personal track recording for iPhone.

Safety first. Svalbard Map is a navigation aid, not a safety or rescue system. Check official notices, carry independent navigation and communications equipment, and make your own route and emergency plan.

Contact

Email info@sdilejslunce.cz for app support, privacy questions, feedback, or feature requests. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the problem. Do not email sensitive location or emergency information.

Common help

My location is not shown

In iPhone Settings, open Privacy & Security > Location Services > Svalbard Map and allow location access. Location is used to show your position and, when you start a recording, to record the track you choose.

Can I record with my screen locked?

Yes. While an active recording is running, the app can continue to receive location updates in the background. Stop the recording when finished. iOS shows its location-use indicator during this use.

How do I use maps offline?

Download the intended area while connected, then test it in Airplane Mode before leaving coverage. Downloaded maps are stored on your iPhone and can be managed from the Offline maps tab.

How do I delete my information?

Delete tracks, saved places and offline map areas in the app. Deleting the app removes its private on-device data, subject to your iOS backup settings.

Map data and rights

Map layers are provided by the Norwegian Polar Institute and are displayed with the required attribution and licence links in the app. Map data are supplied as-is and may not match terrain or be suitable for navigation.
