Privacy policy.

Effective September 13, 2026

Subtle Markdown is a Mac Markdown editor provided by Adam Zemancik. This policy describes the app being prepared for release and its support website.

The app does not send your notes to the developer. It has no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or sign-in service.

Information on your Mac

The app works with files and folders you choose. It stores note recovery drafts, version history, recent-file references, folder-access bookmarks, and preferences locally. These records can include document text, file names and paths, and modification times. They are used to open your files, save your changes, search your library, and help recover writing.

Recovery copies and history are separate from your original files. Removing a folder from the library does not delete the folder, its notes, or existing recovery copies. Saving a recovered draft removes that draft’s recovery journal; unsaved recovery journals may remain until resolved. History uses a rolling retention policy when new versions are recorded, so inactive history may remain in local storage for longer. Local backups can retain their own copies.

File providers and sharing

If you place a note in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or another provider-managed folder, the provider handles synchronization under its own terms and privacy policy. Subtle Markdown does not operate a separate sync service. Files you export, copy, print, or share go to the destination you choose, which may have its own data practices.

Images and external links

Remote images are blocked by default. If you enable remote images in Settings, the app requests images from addresses embedded in your notes. Those hosts receive your network address and information needed to serve the request, and may keep their own logs. You can leave this option off and use local images instead. Links you choose to open are handled by your browser. The app does not control external sites or their privacy practices.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain support records for as long as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Email providers process these messages to deliver and store them. Please avoid sending confidential notes or unnecessary personal information. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided.

Diagnostics

Subtle Markdown does not include a developer-operated crash-reporting service. Apple may process diagnostic information according to your macOS and App Store settings and may make diagnostic reports available to the developer. Review your system privacy settings to control diagnostic sharing.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and processes technical information under its privacy statement. See GitHub’s GitHub Pages documentation for its hosting practices.

Your choices

You choose which files the app can access, whether remote images load, and what you send to support. Your original notes can be managed in Finder. App recovery and history data are stored separately in macOS application storage and may remain after moving or deleting original notes or removing the app. Contact support for help locating and removing these local copies without losing work you want to keep.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app’s data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
