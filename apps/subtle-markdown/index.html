Meet Subtle Markdown

Preparing for release · macOS 14 or later

A little space to think

Begin with a thought.

Give it a sentence. Let that sentence become a paragraph.

There is no perfect first draft.
There is only a place to begin.

A few words, a useful list, an idea for tomorrow. Your notes can stay beautifully simple.

01

Your files, your folders.

Work with Markdown on your Mac. Browse a folder, search your writing, and follow an outline through longer notes.

02

Write the way you think.

Use rich formatting or edit the Markdown source. Choose a comfortable font, a light or dark appearance, and a quieter focus view.

03

Keep the words that matter.

Autosave for saved notes, local draft recovery, and version history help you keep your work. Export HTML or rich text, or print to PDF.

A small app.
A familiar way of working.

No account to create. No separate cloud library to manage. Keep notes in the folders you already use, including folders managed by your preferred file provider.

Subtle Markdown is being prepared for its first release. Availability and an App Store link will appear here when it launches.
