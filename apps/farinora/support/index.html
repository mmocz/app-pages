Farinora

Support for your Italian kitchen.

Farinora helps you calculate and plan pizza dough, focaccia, ciabatta, fresh pasta, and gnocchi recipes on your iPhone.

Need help?

Email Farinora support. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Your saved doughs

Saved setups stay on your iPhone. To remove one, open My doughs in Farinora and swipe left on the setup. Deleting Farinora removes the setups stored by the app on that device.

Privacy

Farinora does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.
