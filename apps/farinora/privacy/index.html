Farinora Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

What Farinora stores

When you save a dough setup, its recipe settings and any note you add are stored only on your device so you can view them later in My doughs.

What Farinora does not do

Farinora does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use your information for tracking. It does not use analytics, advertising networks, accounts, or third-party services.

Your choices

You can delete a saved setup from My doughs at any time. Deleting the app removes the saved setups stored by the app on this device.

Questions

For privacy questions or support, please email Farinora support.
